about 0.98 About Me

Computer Vision Engineer & AI Developer specializing in object detection, image classification, and real-time visual intelligence systems.

Hello, I’m Orhan Burak Turan, a senior Software Engineering student at Manisa Celal Bayar University. I took my first steps in coding at high school in the Database Programming track, where I built C# and SQL-driven applications. Those early projects cemented my passion for software and led me to pursue a degree in Software Engineering.

During my first university years I explored several paths — from full-stack web development to reaching the finals of the Teknofest competition with my team. The rapid progress in AI and computer vision shifted my focus: I decided to dedicate my career to building intelligent visual systems. Currently, I am working at Ergosafe Partner, where I develop computer vision solutions for real-world safety and industrial applications.

Key Projects

  • Plantly – A mobile app that diagnoses plant diseases using a custom-trained CNN model and delivers care advice via LLM. End-to-end development from model training to deployment.
  • MotivAI – A personal trainer app with real-time pose estimation and form-analysis AI using computer vision to check exercise technique and enhance safety.

My goal is to build scalable computer vision pipelines and AI-powered products that solve real-world problems — from industrial safety to healthcare and beyond.
