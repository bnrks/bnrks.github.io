About Me

AI & Machine Learning Developer with hands-on experience in front-end development and intelligent systems.

I'm Orhan Burak Turan, a 3rd-year Software Engineering student at Manisa Celal Bayar University. I’m passionate about artificial intelligence and continuously explore new fields to apply it. I started with C# and SQL in vocational high school and have since expanded into data analysis, machine learning, and web development.

I’ve developed multiple projects involving voice recognition, document analysis, and news recommendation using Python, TensorFlow, and ReactJS. I actively experiment with convolutional neural networks and use technologies such as smolAI, n8n, and NewsAPI to build AI-driven agents.

I also participated in the TEKNOFEST Autonomous Vehicle competition with the POYRAZ Karaçor team, which strengthened my skills in interface development and teamwork under pressure. I value learning-by-doing and embrace collaborative environments where innovative ideas are welcomed.

Beyond academics, I’m a proactive member of my university’s Science and Technology Club, always seeking opportunities to grow and contribute to impactful projects in the field of AI.
